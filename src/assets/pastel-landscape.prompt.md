# Pastel landscape

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the exam library header and card backgrounds.

## Prompt

Create a wide 1536x1024 raster illustration asset for subtle backgrounds behind information cards in an educational web app. A single continuous airy landscape: blush pink layered mountains on the left, pale sky-blue calm sea and gentle curved waves across the lower middle, delicate pale butter-yellow botanical leaves entering from the right edge. Refined soft paper-cut and watercolor style, very light pastel colors, pearlescent white background. Large empty white upper and central areas for UI text; visual details concentrated along the lower third and outer edges. Shapes recognizable but sparse, elegant and quiet, no outlines, no text, no logos, no characters, no sun circles, no orbs, no bokeh. Actual background illustration only, no cards, no UI mockup, no borders.
